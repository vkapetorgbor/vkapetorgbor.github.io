Job Market Paper in preparation

Kept Poor by Design: Colonial Origins of Ghana’s North–South Divide
Uses a spatial regression discontinuity around the 1902 Northern Territories Protectorate boundary to identify the effect of colonial-era underdevelopment policy on present-day human capital and labour market outcomes.

Working Papers

“My sheep hear my voice”: The effect of papal speech on anti-immigration hate crimes in Italy
with Elisabetta Lodigiani
A difference-in-differences design testing whether Pope Francis’ 2013 Lampedusa speech reduced anti-immigration hate crimes in Catholic-leaning Italian municipalities.
Bullied or bullying into tracks? The effect of peer aggression on the choice of high school tracks in Italy
with Giorgio Brunello and Elisabetta Lodigiani
A control-function approach, using INVALSI panel data, examining how primary-school bullying perpetration and victimisation affect academic-track enrolment eight years later.
