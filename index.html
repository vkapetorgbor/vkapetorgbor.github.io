Welcome to my website!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics specialising in applied microeconomics, with particular research interests in economics of education, economics of migration, and applied microeconometrics.

I obtained my Master's Degree in Economics and Data Analysis from the University of Verona in 2022. I am currently a PhD candidate at the University of Padova, Italy (dSEA “Marco Fanno”), and a former Visiting Doctoral Researcher at the Universität Autònoma de Barcelona, Spain.

In my free time, I enjoy composing choral/baroque-era music and jogging.
