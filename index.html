I am a PhD candidate in Economics with research interests in applied microeconomics, economics of education, economics of migration, and applied microeconometrics.

I obtained my MSc in Economics and Data Analysis from the Università degli Studi di Verona in 2022. Currently, I am a PhD candidate at the Università degli Studi di Padova (dSEA “Marco Fanno”), and a Visiting Doctoral Researcher at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona.

I grew up in Ghana, and before pursuing a PhD, I studied and worked in Verona (Italy). In my free time, I enjoy composing music and tutoring students in economics.
